## Power Consumption & Operating Costs

### Electricity Cost Data

#### Digital Signage Power Consumption
- **Source**: Crown TV (2024)
- **Indoor 55-inch LCD**: 150-200 watts per hour
- **Commercial displays larger**: Can reach 200-300+ watts
- **Typical calculation**: 155W × 24 hours × 365 days = 1,357 kWh/year
- **At $0.12/kWh**: ~$163/year per device

#### Raspberry Pi Power Consumption
- **Pi Zero 2 W**: 2-3W typical
- **Pi 4**: 5-7W typical
- **Pi 5**: 8-10W typical
- **Annual cost at $0.12/kWh**:
  - Pi Zero: ~$2-4/year
  - Pi 4: ~$5-8/year
  - Pi 5: ~$8-12/year

## Network Bandwidth Research

### Bandwidth Requirements

#### Typical Dashboard Usage
- **Power BI embed token**: <5 KB per load
- **Report initial load**: 500 KB - 20 MB (depends on complexity)
- **Refresh payload**: 50 KB - 5 MB
- **Logging/telemetry**: ~10 KB per cycle

#### Estimated Monthly Usage
- 60-second refresh interval × 24 hours × 30 days = 43,200 cycles/month
- Average 2 MB per cycle = ~86 GB/month (high estimate)
- More realistic: 1.5 GB/month per device

## Process & Methodology

### Research Approach

1. **Primary Source Priority**: Official vendor/company websites
2. **Secondary Validation**: Industry reports and aggregators
3. **Currency**: Focus on 2024-2025 data (current market conditions)
4. **Cross-Reference**: Verified multiple sources for consistency
5. **Conservative Estimates**: Used lower end of ranges where variance existed
